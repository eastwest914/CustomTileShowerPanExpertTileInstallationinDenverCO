Why a Custom Tile Shower Pan Is Worth It

A custom tile shower pan is a site-built shower floor. Instead of dropping in a prefabricated plastic or fiberglass base, our crew builds the pan layer by layer: a sloped mortar bed, a continuous waterproof membrane, a properly placed drain assembly and finally tile that matches the rest of your shower. The result is a seamless, high-end look with no visible edges between the walls and the floor.

The biggest advantage is freedom. A custom tile shower pan can be any size or shape. Oversized walk-in showers, curbless entries for accessibility, linear drains, corner showers and unusual layouts are all possible. Prefab bases only come in standard dimensions, which often forces compromises. With a custom pan, the shower fits your room instead of the other way around.

Durability is the second benefit. When built correctly, a tiled shower pan can last for decades. The key phrase is "built correctly." The slope must be consistent, usually a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing must be flawless. This is the type of detail work that separates the top shower pan installers from average ones, and it is exactly where Denver Tiles puts its focus.

Shower Base Installation Done Right

Not every project calls for a fully custom pan. Some homeowners prefer a pre-sloped foam shower base or a prefabricated tray, especially when speed and budget matter. Our shower base installation service covers both approaches. We assess your subfloor, plumbing location and goals, then recommend the system that gives you the best balance of performance and cost.

Whichever base you choose, preparation matters. We check that the subfloor is solid and level, repair any damaged framing and confirm that the drain is positioned correctly before a single tile goes down. We also flood test every pan. After the membrane is installed, we plug the drain, fill the pan with water and let it sit for a full day. If the water level holds, we know the waterproofing works. If it drops, we fix the issue before it becomes your problem. Skipping this step is one of the most common reasons showers fail within a few years.

Waterproofing Solutions That Protect Your Home

Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water moves through grout lines and, over time, through the tile itself. What actually keeps your home dry is the waterproofing layer beneath the tile. Our waterproofing solutions use industry-proven systems, including sheet membranes, liquid-applied membranes and bonded waterproof foam boards, depending on the application.

In a shower, we waterproof the entire wet area: the pan, the curb or threshold, the walls up to the showerhead and the niches and benches where water tends to collect. Seams, corners and penetrations such as valves and shower heads receive extra attention with banding and sealant. We follow manufacturer instructions precisely, because even the best membrane fails if it is installed incorrectly.

Waterproofing is not just for showers. Tub surrounds, steam rooms, balconies, kitchen backsplashes and basement floors all benefit from proper moisture control. Denver's dry climate can make homeowners think moisture is not a concern, but plumbing leaks, snowmelt tracked indoors and daily shower use create real risk. A small investment in quality waterproofing prevents expensive repairs later.

Emergency Waterproofing When Leaks Cannot Wait

Sometimes a problem shows up before you expect it. A stain appears on the ceiling beneath your bathroom. The grout around your shower floor stays wet for days. Tiles feel loose or spongy underfoot. These are signs that water is escaping and damaging the structure, and they need quick attention.

Denver Tiles offers emergency waterproofing to stop active leaks and limit damage. We inspect the area, locate the source and explain your options in plain language. Depending on the situation, we may repair the membrane, replace a failed drain connection, rebuild a section of the shower pan or perform a full tear-out and rebuild. Acting fast matters because mold can begin to grow within a couple of days of persistent moisture. If you suspect a leak, call 720-574-0946 right away so we can schedule an inspection.

Best Tile Installation Starts Below the Surface

Anyone can lay tile on a flat floor and make it look nice on day one. What sets the best tile installation apart is what you cannot see: surface preparation, correct thinset coverage, proper trowel selection, movement joints and patient curing times. Large-format tile in particular demands a perfectly flat substrate, and lippage, the uneven edge between neighboring tiles, is a sign of rushed work.

At Denver Tiles, we measure, plan the layout so cuts look balanced and use quality setting materials designed for the specific tile and location. We select the right grout for the job, whether that is cement-based, epoxy or a stain-resistant formula, and we seal natural stone when needed. The finished surface looks crisp and holds up under daily use.
